 ## 🔍 Major Insights
- 🇬🇧 **UK drives 84% of revenue** — 8.2M out of 9.75M total
- ⏰ **Peak sales: 10 AM – 12 PM** (1,691 transactions at peak)
- 📅 **July is top-performing month** at 0.45M; December lowest at 0.23M
- 🏆 **Top product:** DOTCOM Postage, Regency Cakestand, Jumbo Bag
- ⚠️ **27% missing** product descriptions; significant CustomerID gaps flagged
